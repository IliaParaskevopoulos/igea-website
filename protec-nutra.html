OVERVIEW

A traditional fermented ingredient, supplied in the format you need.

Red yeast rice is rice fermented with the yeast Monascus purpureus. It has long been used in Asia and is a well-known ingredient in cardiovascular support supplements.

ProTec Nutra supplies red yeast rice ready for production: as powders, granules, beadlets or filled capsules, in standard and organic grades. That means less processing on your side and a faster route to a finished product.

This page is for business-to-business use by supplement and food manufacturers. Statements about the ingredient summarise published research. They are not health claims for finished products and have not been evaluated by a competent authority. Manufacturers are responsible for checking that their product, dose and labelling meet the rules in each market where they sell. In the EU, food supplements containing monacolins from red yeast rice must follow Regulation (EU) 2022/860, which limits the daily amount and requires specific warnings.
