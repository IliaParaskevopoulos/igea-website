OVERVIEW

Magnesium that is designed to reach the brain.

Most magnesium salts do little to raise magnesium in the brain. Magnesium L-threonate was developed in 2010 by combining magnesium with threonate, a compound naturally present in the body. Research has shown it raises brain magnesium and enters neurons.

Magtein® is the patented, branded form of magnesium L-threonate. It lets brands move beyond generic magnesium with a premium cognition and sleep story backed by human studies.

PRODUCT FEATURES

  • Patented, branded magnesium L-threonate
  • Designed to increase brain magnesium
  • Human RCTs on cognition and sleep
  • Recent 2025 trial from Australia
  • Both components naturally present in the body

THE SCIENCE

Human evidence on cognition and sleep.

Magtein® has been studied in randomised, placebo-controlled trials in adults. The most recent was run in Australia and published in 2025.

RCT · COGNITION & SLEEP · 2025

Magtein® in adults with poor sleep

PARTICIPANTS
100 adults, 18–45
DURATION
6 weeks
DOSE
2 g / day
REFERENCE
[1]

Magtein® improved overall cognition, cognitive age, working memory, reaction time, heart rate and heart rate variability compared with placebo. Some self-reported, but not device-measured, sleep outcomes also improved.

RCT · OLDER ADULTS · 2016

Magnesium L-threonate and cognitive impairment

PARTICIPANTS
Older adults
DURATION
12 weeks
OUTCOME
Composite cognitive score
REFERENCE
[2]

A magnesium L-threonate-containing product improved composite cognitive scores in older adults with cognitive complaints.

PRODUCT FEATURES

  • Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials
  • Benefits on cognition, memory and reaction time
  • Effects on heart rate variability, a marker of stress resilience
  • Studied at a defined 2 g daily dose
