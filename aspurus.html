OVERVIEW

A traditional Ayurvedic herb, studied with modern clinical methods.

Shatavari has been used in Ayurveda for centuries to support women's health. aspurus® brings that tradition into evidence-based formulation: a full-spectrum root extract made with a patent-pending extraction process and studied in randomised, placebo-controlled trials.

For brands, it offers a plant-based, non-hormonal ingredient for the fast-growing menopause and women's health category, effective at a low daily dose that fits in one capsule.

PRODUCT FEATURES

  • Three clinical studies in women
  • Studied at a low dose of 500 mg per day

THE SCIENCE

Clinical evidence across women's life stages.

aspurus® has been studied in randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials. Below is a summary of the main study; full papers are part of the technical file.

RCT · MENOPAUSE · 2024

Shatavari root extract and menopausal symptoms

DESIGN
Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicentre
DURATION
8 weeks
DOSE
250 mg twice daily
REFERENCE
[1]

Compared with placebo, the extract significantly reduced hot flashes, night sweats, insomnia, anxiety, nervousness, vaginal dryness and loss of libido. It was well tolerated, with no significant adverse events.
